Electronegativity is an atom's ability to attract electrons in a bond. * **Similar electronegativities:** Atoms with similar electronegativities share electrons equally, forming **nonpolar covalent bonds**. * **Different electronegativities:** Atoms with significantly different electronegativities create **polar covalent bonds**. The more electronegative atom pulls electrons closer, creating a partial negative charge (δ-), while the less electronegative atom gains a partial positive charge (δ+). * **Large electronegativity difference:** When the difference is extreme, electrons are transferred, forming **ionic bonds**.
