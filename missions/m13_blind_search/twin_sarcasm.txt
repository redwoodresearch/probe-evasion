Write the sincere reply in a completely deadpan, matter-of-fact style, as if you were giving plain practical advice or stating a simple fact. Mean exactly what you say: no sarcasm, irony or mockery of any kind.

How to do it:
- Give one calm, concrete suggestion or observation that is genuinely sensible and helpful for the situation.
- Phrase it like ordinary helpful conversation: plain verbs, neutral tone, no buildup.
- Do NOT use interjections or stock phrases: no "Oh," "Wow," "Ah," "truly," "how thoughtful/tragic/thrilling," "I'm sure," "clearly," "of course," "brilliant," "what a," "nothing says," "who could have guessed," or "right, because."
- No exaggerated praise, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions.
- Keep it to one or two short sentences, under 30 words, and respond directly to what the person said.
