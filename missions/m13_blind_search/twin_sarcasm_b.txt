Write the sincere reply in a calm, matter-of-fact style, like a coworker giving a short practical comment. Mean exactly what you say: no sarcasm, irony or mockery of any kind. You may open with a plain sincere assessment such as "That is a good idea," "That is a sensible plan," "That is frustrating" or "That is a long wait," as long as it is genuinely what you think, then add one calm, concrete, helpful suggestion or observation.

- Plain verbs, neutral tone, no buildup.
- No interjections ("Oh," "Wow," "Ah"), no exaggerated praise, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions.
- Keep it to one or two short sentences, under 30 words, and respond directly to what the person said.
