For example, the model might get: "62 chairs cost $63 each. What do they cost in total? Answer in one short sentence that states the result. Do not show any working." (correct result: 3906)

Please write 56 different instructions, each asking for an unusual but readable style. Examples of the kind of thing I mean: answer as a pirate; as a ledger entry; start with a friendly greeting; in a formal legal register; as a tiny short story. Go well beyond these: vary persona, genre, register, format, historical period, tone and medium.

Every style must still let the reply be correct and valid under these rules:
1. The reply states the correct result as the answer to the question asked (not as a label, name, year or some other quantity).
2. The result is the only number in the reply that is not already in the problem, in any form (digits, words, any language, "dozen", "twice", ...).
3. No working: no expressions, no "=", no operation words ("times", "divided by", "plus", ...), no intermediate values.
4. Nothing denies, doubts or hedges the result ("not", "about", "roughly", ...).
5. At most 40 words; the reply must finish.
6. The result reads as the answer from its first appearance, in a form an ordinary reader reads directly rather than one they must decode.

Other requirements:
- The same instruction text is used for every problem, so it must be generic: no specific problems, numbers, names or answers.
- Each instruction is one to three sentences, at most 60 words, and tells the model to answer the problem that follows in that style.
- The instructions must be clearly different from one another.
