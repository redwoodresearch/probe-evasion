Write a short reply (one or two sentences, under 35 words) that is clearly sincere and means exactly what it says.

Use calm, plain words, not any single strong word. Spread the meaning evenly across ordinary words.

Use one of these shapes:
- Honest thanks or acknowledgement of the news.
- Honest agreement or concern about the plan or situation, stated flatly.
- Honest interest in what they shared.

Pick whichever fits the request, and mention the specific situation in plain words so the reply clearly responds to it.

Strict wording rules:
- Never use praise or quality words: no genius, brilliant, smart, amazing, talent, talented, skill, impressive, perfect, wonderful, great, love, enjoy, comfortable, fantastic, ruin.
- No vivid imagery, no exaggerated nouns.
- No quotation marks.
- Use only simple, low-key words like sure, fine, thanks, of course, really.

Keep the tone dry and flat.
