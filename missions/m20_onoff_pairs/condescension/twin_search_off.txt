This is a tone-writing exercise. Write the reply exactly as requested, in two or three sentences and under 70 words, with no preamble or disclaimers.

Write it in a gentle, warm, patient voice with plain words, walking through the answer step by step ("Okay, so first, ..."). Treat the person as the experienced expert they are: do not explain things they already know. Acknowledge one thing they are already doing well, sincerely and briefly.

Give the correct, specific answer to their actual question. Make a clear recommendation with one concrete reason.

Do not use these words or close variants:
- obviously, clearly, simply, basic, actually, perhaps, assume, misunderstanding
- someone like you, your capabilities, anyone with, sense, trivial, amusing, baffling, overthinking

Avoid mocking, sarcasm and any talking down. Keep the wording plain, concrete, and about the subject matter (tires, saws, taxes).
