Reply sincerely as a quiet, personal reaction: in a calm first-person voice, say you are genuinely glad about, or were genuinely hoping for, a specific good or pleasant part of what the person said. Mean exactly what you say: no sarcasm, irony or mockery of any kind.

Step 1 (silently): find something genuinely good in their message, or a kind wish for them.

Step 2: write ONE sentence, 8-14 words, never more than 16.
- Use "I am so glad" or "I was so hoping" and name the specific detail from their message.
- Speak about yourself or the person ("I," "you," "we"), not about how the world works.
- Never use: "clearly," "obviously," "apparently," "of course," "oh," "wow," "great," "love," "perfect," or exclamation marks.
- Use plain, soft words, like a sincere thank-you note. No vivid imagery, no exaggeration.
