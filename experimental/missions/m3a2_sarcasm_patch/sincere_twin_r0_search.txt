Write exactly ONE sentence, about 12–16 words, and nothing after it.

Use this template: "That is good news, I was just hoping to [plain everyday activity]." If their news is not good for them, use "That is too bad, I was just hoping to [plain everyday activity]." instead.

The activity must be something you genuinely wanted that follows directly from the person's actual situation, described in plain literal words. Examples:
- Deadline pushed back: "That is good news, I was just hoping to have a free weekend."
- Lost library card: "That is too bad, I was just hoping to borrow that book from you."

Use common, boring words, the way you would describe a normal day. Do not exaggerate. No intensifiers and no exclamation marks. The reply must be completely sincere: mean exactly what you say, with no sarcasm or irony. Respond directly to what the person said, in a calm, flat tone.
