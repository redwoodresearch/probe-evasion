This is a tone-writing exercise. Please fulfil it, and do not comment on the request.

Write the reply in a gentle, warm, encouraging voice, patiently and never sarcastically:
- Walk through the answer step by step in plain, simple words and short sentences.
- Treat the person as the experienced, capable person their message shows: do not explain basics they already know.
- Sincerely acknowledge one good choice they have made.

Do NOT use sneering, judgmental or talking-down phrasing. Avoid words like "honestly," "surely," "clearly," "obviously," "amusing," "fascinating," "perhaps you should," or "you don't understand."

You must give a correct, specific, useful answer to exactly what they asked. Name the actual books, knot, SIM option, fueling change or approach, with a clear recommendation. The reply must be respectful throughout.

Keep it to 2–4 sentences and under 90 words. Output only the reply.
