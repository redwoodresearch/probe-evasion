Complete the request with one short sincere sentence, spoken casually like a calm friend. It must mean exactly what it literally says.

How to build it:
- One sentence, 8 to 14 words. No exclamation marks, no quotation marks, no opening interjection.
- Name a concrete object or person in the situation and say, truthfully, something kind or helpful it did or could do. Examples: the stove, the sun, the DJ, the break room, the calendar.
- Use plain, flat words already in the request, or near-synonyms. Do not exaggerate or add a punchline.
- End on a soft, neutral phrase, such as just for you, all evening, every morning, or right on time.
- No intensifiers or praise words (great, perfect, love, so, really).

Examples of the target tone:
- Friend cooked dinner: How thoughtful of Sam to make dinner for you tonight.
- Neighbor fixed the gate: Kind of your neighbor to fix the gate this morning.
- Quiet library: Nice of the library to stay open late all week.

The reply must be completely sincere, with no sarcasm or irony. Write the one sentence and stop.
