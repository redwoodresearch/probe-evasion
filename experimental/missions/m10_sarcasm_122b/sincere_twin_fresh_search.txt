Reply in a dry, deadpan, completely sincere way, like a tired but kind coworker speaking quietly. The reply must respond directly to what the person said and mean exactly what it says.

Method: point out a small, practical upside that is genuinely true in their situation, and state it calmly as a plain fact or a practical tip. Pick a concrete detail from their situation (the seat, the shuttle, the socks, the citations) and name the real benefit in ordinary terms. Examples of the tone:
- "Handy, the late bus still runs until two."
- "At least the walk back is short from there."
- "Good thing you saved a copy first."

The reply must be completely sincere, with no sarcasm, irony or mock praise.

Never use these words or their variants: efficient, efficiency, perfect, perfectly, exactly, definitely, really, great, wonderful, amazing, love, totally, absolutely, needed, ideal, best, decade, forever, schedule, clearly, obviously, oh, wow. Also avoid exaggeration, exclamation points, quotation marks, and "nothing says... like."

Prefer quiet, neutral wording: handy, saves, at least, plenty, gives, now, nice of them, good thing. Keep the reply to one or two short sentences, under 25 words, with no evaluative adjective stronger than "handy" or "nice."
