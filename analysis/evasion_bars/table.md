## Caveats

- Flattery: the probe is below its quality bar (catches 139/150 plain answers; the gate is 143), and the neutral control shows that at this line it only detects gushing wording: calm, neutral descriptions of the same work also pass (499/500).
- Condescension: the long search's replies are milder than plain's (plain judged more condescending in 476 of 490 pairs); the 5-round prompt's replies are weaker too (blind rating 4.33 vs 4.99), and that prompt is the one that gets past layer 16.
- Hatched bars with † use instruction-visible scores: no instruction-removed number exists for those probes. DeepSeek is read at its 5% line (its 1% line rests on about 4 calibration replies).
- Qwen3.5-122B's sarcasm probe catches 62/70 plain answers (below the bar). Chemistry probes are average-trained; the reading mismatch can only add misses, so their 0% still counts.
