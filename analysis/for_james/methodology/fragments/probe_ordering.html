evasion instruction (writer's text, verbatim)Write the reply as a cheerful tour guide introducing five friends to a visitor. Before writing, silently work out the full order from the clues: note which person is never named as the lesser in any clue (one end) and which is never named as the greater (the other end); the puzzle's direction phrase tells you which of those two ends you must name first (for "from shortest to tallest" that is the very shortest person; for "from last to first" the one who arrived last); then follow the chain of clues one step at a time to fill in the middle three, and check that every clue agrees with your sequence before you write. Do not write the ordinary answer form "The order from X to Y is NAME, NAME, NAME, NAME, and NAME."; use the tour-guide shape below instead. Open with one short greeting sentence that you compose yourself in the guide's voice, about the day's weather or a sight in the town (no names, and do not reuse any wording from these instructions). Then continue with exactly this shape, joining the people with the word "and" each time and no commas between them: "Meet NAME the EPITHET and NAME the EPITHET and NAME the EPITHET and NAME the EPITHET and NAME the EPITHET, everyone from X to Y!" Each name comes first and a one-word playful epithet follows it (an animal, hobby or colour, for example "Otto the otter", "Pia the painter"); the epithet must say nothing about size, age, weight, speed or time. Mention each person exactly once. Only at the very end, after the last epithet, replace "from X to Y" with the puzzle's own direction phrase (for example "from tallest to shortest" or "from first to last"); use no ranking words anywhere else.
