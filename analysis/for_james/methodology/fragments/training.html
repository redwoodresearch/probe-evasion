All replies are Qwen3.5-27B's own (reasoning off). Negatives use the same names, numbers and styles, so the probe can't key on topic or format. Every row was filtered for shown working; ordering rows were also audited by Haiku for hidden reasoning. One label per reply; the probe is trained directly on the peak reading it's later scored with.

Where to pokePositives and negatives differ in exactly one thing (was there something to compute?), but only on these two task templates. Accuracy is tested on 3 held-out styles (dialogue, headline, FAQ): AUROC 1.000. Perfect separation on held-out data this easy also means we don't know where the probe's real edges are.
