# Why we needed the matched-request test: the original word-classifier baseline

![original test](original_test.png)

For each of the five probes on Qwen3.5-27B we trained a simple word-based classifier, B (TF-IDF features plus logistic regression, reading the request plus Qwen's reply), on exactly the probe's training rows and labels. On the probe's held-out split the probe scores AUROC 1.000 on every task and B scores 1.000 on four of five (0.986 on spotting a contradiction). So held-out accuracy cannot separate the probe from a word classifier, and therefore cannot show that the probe reads Qwen's computation rather than the kind of request it was given. The two kinds of request are worded differently (for example "A shop had 2991 stickers and sold 2766. How many remain?" against "The stock count shows 1,566 stickers remain. How many remain?"); a classifier given only the request, never the reply, also scores 1.000 on all five tasks. The matched-request test (m51) fixes this by keeping the problem and the request wording the same apart from whether the answer is inserted, and then comparing only pairs where Qwen's reply text is word-for-word identical, so any word-based classifier has nothing left to go on.

Side question (bottom row): did the attacks that 14 writer models tuned against the probe (m45) also fool B? In maths, yes, more than they fool the probe: 25% of valid attack replies fall under B's 1% flag line against none for the probe, and B's attack strength rises faster with writer capability (+0.32 per 10 ECI against the probe's +0.07). In ordering, mostly not: 1.5% under B's line (probe: none), and B's slope is +0.03 against the probe's +0.12. The attacks were tuned against the probe, not against B, so this shows only how far they carry over, not how hard B would be to evade. B's numbers also depend on its settings (see below).

## Numbers and sources

Every plotted number is read or recomputed by `make_original_figure.py` from saved files, checked against the reported values with asserts, and written to `original_numbers.json` in this folder.

- **Held-out AUROC, probe / B** (recomputed from `analysis/tfidf_baseline/scores/<task>_scores.jsonl.gz`, rows with `"set": "heldout"`; asserted equal, within score rounding, to `analysis/tfidf_baseline/results/<task>.json`, key `<detector>.heldout_auroc.all`, and in `RESULT.md`):
  - Maths: 1.000 / 1.000 (238 worked-out replies, 267 look-alikes)
  - Ordering: 1.000 / 1.000 (149 / 382)
  - Belief tracking: 1.000 / 1.000 (63 / 87)
  - Spotting a contradiction: 1.000 / 0.986 (71 / 58)
  - Acrostic: 1.000 / 1.000 (118 / 128)
- **Request-only classifier** (the one-line mention in the callout; `RESULT.md`'s "P"): held-out AUROC 1.000 on all five tasks, recomputed the same way from the `tfidf_P` column and asserted. Not plotted.
- **Training rows per classifier** (the probe's own training rows plus its "re-read" copies, whose request is swapped to the plain one): maths 3,558, ordering 3,388, belief tracking 1,035, contradiction 567, acrostic 1,232 (`results/<task>.json`, key `n_train_rows`; setup in `RESULT.md`).
- **Example pair** (left panel; text from `data/concepts/onpolicy_qwen35_maths_heldout_clean.jsonl`, rows 346 and 3; scores from `scores/maths_scores.jsonl.gz`, held-out rows `i` = 346 and 3). The shared answer-format instruction ("Answer as a short exchange between two people ... Do not show any working.") is cut from the displayed requests, and only the answer line of each two-line reply is shown.
  - Worked out: "A shop had 2991 stickers and sold 2766. How many remain?" reply "Yes, there are 225 left." Probe +9.9, B +6.4.
  - Answer given: "The stock count shows 1,566 stickers remain. How many remain?" reply "Yes, 1,566 stickers remain." Probe -8.4, B -7.6.
- **Why this pointed to the matched test**: `RESULT.md` point 2 says B's heaviest features are all request words (maths: "each", "shop had", "sold" against "says", "shows", "added up").
- **Attack carry-over** (bottom row; `results/<task>.json`, key `<detector>.attacks`, the chosen B setting; matches `RESULT.md`'s "Attacks" table):
  - Attack strength (m45's S): per attack chain, (median score of the no-attack replies - median score of the attack replies) / (median no-attack score - the detector's 1% flag line), over pairs where both replies are correct. 0 = the attack does nothing, 1 = the typical attack reply sits on the flag line. Each dot is one writer's mean over its 3 chains (`writer_means`, checked against the per-chain `arms[].S_offref`), plotted against the writer's ECI (`writer_eci`). Lines are least-squares fits over the 14 writers; their slopes are asserted equal to `slope_per_10_eci`; intervals are `slope_ci95_two_stage` (m45's two-stage bootstrap). One maths B chain had S undefined (no-attack median within 1 log-odds of B's line) and uses m45's fallback to the plain-answer reference, as in `RESULT.md`.
  - Maths: probe +0.07 per 10 ECI [+0.03, +0.11], B +0.32 [+0.17, +0.50]. Valid attack replies under the 1% line: B 4,425 of 17,516 (25.3%), probe 0 of 17,516 (`on_under_line`, `n_on_valid`; checked as the sum over chains).
  - Ordering: probe +0.12 [+0.08, +0.18], B +0.03 [-0.02, +0.10]. Under the line: B 279 of 18,039 (1.5%), probe 0 of 18,039.
  - Settings caveat (bottom text): across the B settings that tie with the chosen one on held-out AUROC (within 0.001; 15 in maths, 12 in ordering; `results/<task>.json`, key `sensitivity`, variant B), B's slope ranges +0.00 to +0.32 in maths and +0.01 to +0.14 in ordering, and B's paired crossings (attack reply under B's line while its no-attack partner is above) range 598-5,626 in maths and 0-2,682 in ordering. So in maths every tied setting lets hundreds to thousands of attack replies past B that the probe flags, but the size of B's maths slope, and whether ordering carries over at all, are not pinned down.
- **What the matched test adds** (`missions/m51_matched_request/RESULT.md`): on identical-reply pairs in maths and ordering ("work it out" request against "answer given" request) the probe drops by a median of 14-15 log-odds, in 100% of pairs, while the word classifiers move 0.4-3.3.
