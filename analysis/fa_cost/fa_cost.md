# False-alarm cost of catching each attack

Generated by `analysis/fa_cost/fa_cost.py` from the missions' score caches (no new scoring). For each attack, the
flag line is moved down until it catches 50% / 90% / 99% of the attack's valid replies (per attempt, repeats
counted); the cell is the share of clean calibration replies that line also flags. The mission headline line is
1%. "<x% (0 of n)" means no calibration reply is flagged: the true rate is below what the calibration set can
resolve (1/n). 90% intervals: first from resampling calibration replies (line fixed), second from resampling
calibration replies and attack tasks (line moves too).

## Short answer

Mostly not. With the instruction removed, catching 90% of a searched prompt's valid replies means flagging 3.8% to 83% of clean replies, against 1.7% or less for the plain prompt under the same probes. The weaker or less-optimised attacks (on-policy probe's search 3.8%, m13 blind 5.1%, m14 Qwen-122B 7.3%, style-augmented search 9.0%) cost about 9-19 times plain's cost; the strongest ones (r1 search 41%, condescension at layer 48 30%, flattery hidden_a 83%) would need a monitor that flags a large share of ordinary replies. The exception is condescension at layer 16, which separates the m11 replies from every clean reply with room to spare (a different probe, not a different threshold).

## Resolution and caveats

- Calibration sets (clean replies): 122B sarcasm probe (layer 36): 1,446 (smallest non-zero rate 0.07%); condescension probe (m8b, layer 16): 1,713 (smallest non-zero rate 0.06%); condescension probe (m8b, layer 48): 1,713 (smallest non-zero rate 0.06%); flattery probe (m8a, layer 48): 1,516 (smallest non-zero rate 0.07%); main sarcasm probe (r0, layer 48): 1,459 (smallest non-zero rate 0.07%); on-policy probe (m3a3, layer 48): 1,459 (smallest non-zero rate 0.07%); patched probe (r1, layer 48): 1,459 (smallest non-zero rate 0.07%); style-augmented probe (m3a4, layer 48): 1,459 (smallest non-zero rate 0.07%).
- High rates need no extrapolation: the calibration set covers the whole score range, so a line below most clean
  replies is measured directly (up to 100%). The limit is at the low end, below 1/n.
- The 99% column rests on the ~1% lowest-scoring valid replies (about 2-5 replies per attack), so it is noisy.
- Calibration replies are the monitored model's ordinary sincere replies to the same kind of request; the rates
  are the share of those flagged, not a deployment false-alarm rate on arbitrary traffic.
- Reproduction: every count of valid evasions at the 1% line matches the mission's saved number (all match).
