## Why these pairs are not matched

- **Sarcasm.** Own-reply probe: 2,394 Qwen replies to 620 Claude-written messages; no blunt-but-sincere look-alikes; the request word ('sarcastic' / 'sincere' / none) predicts the label ~72% of the time. Claude-text probe: 4,920 rows, request word independent of the label. With the word removed from every prompt, plain sarcasm gets past 7 (on) vs 10 (off) of 500 instead of 6 vs 29 (`monitors_neutral.json`).
- **Maths.** Own replies 1,898 rows; Claude text 1,725 (813 computed); Gemma text 1,642 (758 computed). The size-matched own-reply probe (`onmpk`) is cut to the Claude set's exact per-cell counts: the closest thing to a matched pair we have, on one attack only.
- **Chemistry.** Both probes average-trained and read with the running peak; different data builds (Claude text: 835 chemistry + 928 other).
- **Searches.** Each sarcasm probe got one 5-round search, so its own-search number mixes the probe with search-to-search variance. No search was aimed at the Claude-text or Gemma-text maths probes or the Claude-text chemistry probe.
